# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-based. Welcome participants of every background and experience level. Explain technical disagreements with reproducible examples, and distinguish evidence from opinion.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, disclosure of private information, and deceptive benchmark reporting are unacceptable. Ask before sharing another person's private data or correspondence.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and allow correction where appropriate.

Report a community concern to the repository maintainer through the contact options on [the maintainer profile](https://github.com/vinsymoyya). Avoid publishing personal information in public issues. For software vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
